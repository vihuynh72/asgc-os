# Launch Runbook

## Onboarding
- Confirm allowlist domains and admin emails are set.
- Create current term and assign roles.
- Configure office locations + requirements.
- Seed committees and verify meeting creation.

## Weekly Operations Checklist
- Review office hours compliance report.
- Review upcoming meetings and agenda items.
- Review finance requests and approvals.
- Review ICC attendance and quorum flags.
- Review club eligibility status.

## Monthly Operations Checklist
- Run backups and complete restore checklist.
- Review audit logs and role assignments.
- Archive prior term records if applicable.

## Support + Incident Playbook
- Triage: capture issue, user impact, time, and repro steps.
- Contain: revoke access or pause admin actions if needed.
- Fix: deploy a patch and document root cause.
- Follow-up: update runbook or add safeguards.

## Contacts
- ASGC Advisor (primary)
- VP Finance (finance approvals)
- ICC President (clubs + ICC)
